## Why Use This

Google Business Profile Manager is powerful, but getting quick answers about your local search performance means navigating dashboards, filtering by location, and exporting data manually. This MCP server cuts through all of that.

**What you get:**

- **Instant local presence insights** — ask about searches, views, calls, and direction requests without digging through Business Profile Manager.
- **No setup complexity** — no Google My Business API credentials needed; connect through Two Minute Reports in minutes.
- **Multi-location support** — analyze performance across all your business locations from a single conversation.
- **Cross-platform context** — combine with 22+ other sources like Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads to connect local visibility with website traffic and ad performance.
